# Strategy 3 v4 research run

Run `python tools/strategy3_v4.py --database instance/system.db --output backtesting_results/strategy3_v4.json` from the repository root. The runner rebuilds indicators, ranks with the version 2 rules, and publishes event studies for 2022–2023 development and 2024–2025 validation. The older `tools/strategy3_experiment.py` encodes the previous 2022–2024 protocol and is not the v4 validation protocol; its old results require their original feature implementation.

The benchmark and the survivor universe use Nifty 500. The current constituent CSV is applied to past dates, so historical results have accepted survivorship bias. Validation years were viewed in prior research and are labelled `validation_previously_viewed`, not blind out-of-sample data. The 2026 period remains exposed.

The score is `sum(stock log return − fitted beta × Nifty 500 log return) / standard deviation(residual returns)` over 63 sessions. Beta uses the earlier, non-overlapping 252-session window. The estimated regression intercept is excluded from the score. V4 then requires the top 20% of the liquid cross-section, prior-session Bollinger width percentile at most 25%, prior 20-session mean volume below prior 252-session mean volume, a close crossing above the upper band, and current volume above 1.5 times prior 50-session mean volume. Liquidity requires prior 30-session average `close × volume` above ₹100 million. This traded-value measure is an OHLCV proxy.

At the next exchange open, gaps above 3% and opens at or below the signal-day lower band are skipped. The lower band is the fixed initial stop. Stop-aware horizon returns use the open on a gap below the stop, or the stop price if the day's low reaches it. Daily bars cannot reveal intraday fill quality. A 50 bps round-trip cost is subtracted from each complete outcome as an assumption. The first-cross 252-session high baseline uses the same universe, liquidity gate, next-open execution, stop, and cost assumption.

The descriptive validation gate requires, **in each of 2024 and 2025**: at least 30 complete 20-session events for both strategy and baseline, positive mean 20-session net return above the baseline mean, lower 5-session 90th-percentile maximum adverse excursion, and a lower 5-session failed-breakout rate. The report also retains raw signal and execution counts, gross returns, maximum favorable excursion, and reason funnels. Passing this gate is not a significance claim.

`size_v4_position` calculates whole-share quantity from 1% nominal equity risk, 10% equity per-name cap, 1% of ADV30 order cap, and available cash. Event studies have no shared cash ledger; they do not enforce simultaneous 100% gross exposure or claim portfolio returns. That cap belongs in a later portfolio simulation.
