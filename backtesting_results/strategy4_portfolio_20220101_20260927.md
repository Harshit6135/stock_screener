There were **430 modeled buys and 415 modeled sells**, leaving **15 marked-to-market positions** open at the cutoff. Of 415 closed trades, 167 were profitable (40.24% win rate); the mean closed-trade return was +3.36%, median −3.30%, and profit factor 1.37. Total modeled costs were **₹20,904.38**. Average held positions were 14.05. Seven entry signals generated on the final session remain unfilled because no following bar is available.

The simulator calculates Donchian, Wilder ADX/ATR, and Supertrend from historical daily OHLCV. Signals are formed at the close. Exits due to a close below Supertrend or the prior 20-session low execute at the next available stock open; exits process before entries. Entry orders are sorted by ADX, then traded value, then symbol. A gap above 3% or an open at or below the initial stop anchor skips the entry. Shares are rounded down after risk, stock-value, traded-value, and cash caps. Holdings are marked at the daily close; missing held-stock bars retain the last observed close.

**Interpretation limits:** The earlier fixed-horizon event-study validation gate did not pass, so this result is exploratory. The backtest applies today's Nifty 500 membership to past dates, introducing survivorship bias; 498 of 500 EQ ISINs match stock bars. Per research scope, stored bars are used without corporate-action adjustment. Daily OHLCV cannot prove that a market-on-open order would fill during a price circuit or capture actual slippage. Open holdings are marked to market rather than force-sold on the cutoff date. The 2024–2025 period was previously viewed during strategy research, and 2026 is exposed rather than a blind holdout.
