Open holdings annualized return (XIRR)
—

Uses remaining buy lots and purchase dates. Excludes cash and closed trades.

Whole account annualized return (XIRR)
—

Uses opening capital, dated deposits and withdrawals, and current account value including cash. Buys and sells stay within the account.

Annualized gain (CAGR)
—

Growth from opening capital to current account value, including cash. With later deposits or withdrawals, use whole account XIRR instead.

Absolute return
—

Total net gain divided by net contributed capital. Not annualized.

Realised XIRR
—

Annualized return on sold FIFO lots, using their buy costs and net sale proceeds. Excludes open holdings and idle cash; includes recorded trade charges.

Capital risk
—

Invested capital minus holdings value at trailing stops. Negative means the combined stops are above invested capital; execution prices can differ.
